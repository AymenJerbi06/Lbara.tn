What this expanded view tells you

Annual plans dominate the value math. Disney+ Standard annual at 47 TND beats monthly at 56 TND for the same product. PlayStation Plus 12-month Premium at 629 TND total saves the customer 35% vs paying monthly. Push annual plans hard in your marketing — your fulfillment cost is the same but margin per customer is much higher.

Apple One is genuinely better than separate iCloud+ for many Tunisians. The Family tier at 101 TND covers Music + TV+ + Arcade + 200GB iCloud for a household — better than buying iCloud+ alone (14 TND) plus separate Apple Music subs (~30 TND each). Promote this bundle.

LinkedIn Premium has a massive price ceiling. Career at 119 TND is approachable; Recruiter Lite at 808 TND is enterprise-only. Don't list all six on your front page — that range looks confusing. Lead with Career and Business; offer the higher tiers as "by request."

Twitch Turbo is interesting positioning. At 49 TND for Tunisian gamers who watch a lot of streams, this is a legitimate "I'm tired of ads" purchase — and there's no Spotify-style alternative for it.
