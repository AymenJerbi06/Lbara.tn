Follow the Right Activation Steps

Once your payment is confirmed, our team follows the method selected for that product. Gift-card products may offer code delivery or assisted redemption. Giftable subscriptions only need the email linked to your service account. Card-only services may require existing-account access or a new account created with an email you control.

Gift card / store credit

Shown only for products that support gift cards or store credit. We may send the code, redeem it for you, or guide Apple App Store / Google Play credit setup.

Giftable subscription

Shown only for services that allow subscriptions to be gifted. You provide the email linked to your existing service account.

Account setup

Shown only when the service must be paid for directly by card. You may be asked for an existing account or an email for a new account.

Once your payment is confirmed, our team manually reviews and delivers your credentials by email — usually within 2 hours. You'll also see your order status update in real time.
